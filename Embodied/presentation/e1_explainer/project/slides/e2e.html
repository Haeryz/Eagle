The released model end to end. Image (COCO val2017 000000368335, 411 x 640) goes through MoonViT at native resolution: 30 x 46 patches of 14 px, merged 2 x 2 into 345 image tokens of 4608 dims. The MLP projector maps them to 2048, and they replace the image placeholder embeddings in the prompt. Qwen2.5-3B (36 layers) plus the tied LM head produce logits over 152,681 tokens, 1001 of which are coordinate tokens on a 0-1000 grid. Everything inside the dashed boundary is neural and unchanged. The decoding loop outside it turns logits into committed tokens and feeds them back for the next forward. Output: one fixed 6-token block per box. Placeholder: the grey panels stand in for the COCO image; the parsed boxes are drawn at their true positions.

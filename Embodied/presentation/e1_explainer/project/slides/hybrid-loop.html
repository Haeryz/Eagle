3 / 7

Everything here is outside the Qwen boundary, which is shrunk to the left strip. Per block: (1) sampling transforms on the 6 x V logits; (2) the release block decoder: a heuristic frame check, then the best coordinate per slot if one is in its top 4 (otherwise the raw sampled tokens, which can be malformed); (3) the uncertain-coordinate rule: top-1 below 0.9 and top-4 coordinates spread over more than 60 grid units flags a coordinate and cuts the block there; (4) the router commits a full box, point, empty box, label or end, or rejects the box. A rejected box commits only the start and the good leading coordinates and switches to NTP: one token per forward until the box end, then back to MTP. Two causes: malformed frames, under 1% of blocks; uncertain coordinates, at least 96% of all fallbacks (E0, macro means). Detail: the empty check also requires P(box) of at least 0.7 at slot 0.
